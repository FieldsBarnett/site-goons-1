# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Atlanta-area homeowners planning residential remodeling projects—kitchens, bathrooms, home additions, and custom renovations—who are comparing local general contractors and want a reliable crew they can reach directly without corporate runaround.

## Product Purpose

SiteGoons is a residential general contracting company serving the Atlanta area. The website introduces the company, showcases residential remodeling capabilities (kitchens, baths, additions, custom renovations), and converts visiting homeowners into quote requests via an estimate form, phone call, or email.

## Positioning

A hands-on, approachable Atlanta residential contracting crew that literally shows its work in action—pairing real construction craft with playful, memorable personality (animated construction "goon" characters actively building, painting, and leveling the website itself) so homeowners immediately remember SiteGoons over generic, interchangeable contractor sites.

## Operating Context

Homeowners typically research contractors on phones and laptops at home, comparing past work, scope clarity, and responsiveness before calling or submitting a quote request. Direct contact happens via phone (`205-863-7564`), email (`fields@sitegoons.com`), or a quick on-page quote request form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: Vite + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4, deployed as a static site (Cloudflare Pages).
- Service focus: Residential general contracting only (kitchens, bathrooms, additions, custom renovations) in the Atlanta area.
- Contact channels: On-page quote request form (client-side confirmation / mailto fallback since this is a static site unless a backend endpoint is added later), direct phone (`205-863-7564`), and direct email (`fields@sitegoons.com`).
- Do **not** display contractor license numbers or licensing claims at this time.
- Do **not** fabricate testimonials, real client names, years in business, or commercial claims; any illustrative sample projects or example estimates must be clearly labeled as sample work until real project photos and details are supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: **SiteGoons**.
- Signature interactive motif (binding): Animated little construction "goon" characters actively doing construction on the site itself—such as painting the background or buttons, leveling borders, measuring, or installing UI elements.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed contact details: Phone `205-863-7564`, Email `fields@sitegoons.com`, Service area: Atlanta, GA metro area.
- Absent (do not fabricate): Real completed project photography, client testimonials, license numbers, and pricing guarantees. Sample project cards or scope examples must be labeled as sample/illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. **Direct and reachable:** Phone, email, and quote request are effortless to find and use from anywhere on the page.
2. **Craft with personality:** The animated construction goons are integrated into the real UI structure, reinforcing craftsmanship and charm without obscuring copy or blocking actions.
3. **Honest proof:** Show residential remodeling expertise clearly and label illustrative sample projects transparently until real photography is added.
4. **Built for homeowners:** Plain-spoken residential scope (kitchens, baths, additions, renovations) with clear process steps and zero contractor jargon.
