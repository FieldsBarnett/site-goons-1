# Surface Brief — SiteGoons Landing Page (`src/App.jsx`)

- **Scope:** Primary landing page (`src/App.jsx`, `index.html`, `src/index.css`)
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience:** Atlanta-area homeowners planning residential kitchens, bathrooms, home additions, and custom renovations.
- **Job / Primary Action:** Understand SiteGoons' residential remodeling focus in seconds, experience the playful animated construction goons working on the site itself, and request a quote via the inline form, phone (`205-863-7564`), or email (`fields@sitegoons.com`).
- **Constraints:** Residential only; Atlanta area; no license details; no fabricated testimonials or claims; sample projects clearly labeled as sample work; animated construction goons doing real construction on the UI (painting buttons/background, leveling borders, sawing seams, hoisting blocks).
- **Critique Reference Comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/challenger-cloud-quarry.png` (code-led session flipped on the decision page).

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** SiteGoons turns a residential contractor landing page into a sunlit architectural cut-block quarry where animated construction goons actively saw seams, hoist the quote block, level borders, and roll paint onto the primary button — refusing the generic dark-navy-and-orange stock-photo contractor hero.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Cumulus White (`#F5F7F9`) chamfered architectural blocks parted by deep luminous Atlanta Sky Void (`#4DA7E6` to `#0D2E5E`), Saw Silver (`#D6DADF`) technical seams, Depth Blue (`#0D2E5E`) stencil display ink (`Saira Stencil One`), industrial sans (`Barlow`), and tabular mono (`JetBrains Mono`) for measurements, animated by hardhat SiteGoons in yellow (`#F4B829`) and workwear blue.
- **STORY:** A visiting Atlanta homeowner immediately sees SiteGoons builds residential kitchens, bathrooms, additions, and custom renovations, smiles at the goons painting and leveling the live UI, trusts the plain-spoken process and transparent sample scopes, and submits a quote request or calls `205-863-7564` / emails `fields@sitegoons.com`.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed edge-to-edge top chamfered nav bar (`SITEGOONS`, `Services`, `Sample Work`, `Process`, `Contact`, `205-863-7564`) over a deep sky-blue void parting two chamfered Cumulus White blocks: left block carries the `SITEGOONS — ATLANTA RESIDENTIAL CONTRACTING` stencil headline, scope summary, direct phone/email, primary `Request a Quote` + `Sample Work` buttons, and a goon sawing the diagonal silver seam; right block is a hoisted working `Request a Quote` panel (Kitchen/Bath/Addition/Renovation toggles, Neighborhood, Contact, painted `Get Estimate` button) tended by a crane-hoist goon, a border-leveling goon, and a button-painting goon; the four residential service blocks peek cleanly below the fold.
